# QUIZ TOGETHER 썸네일 — 추가 프롬프트 (O/X 크기·울타리 재고정, 9차 수정)

아래 프롬프트를 그대로 이미지 생성 AI에 붙여넣으시면 됩니다.

*(직전 시도에서 두 가지가 잘못됨: ①남자를 멀리 보내라는 지시 때문에 O/X 자체가 작아지고 여백이 늘어남 → 이번엔 "O/X는 프레임을 꽉 채운 채 크기 그대로 고정"을 맨 앞에 강하게 못박음. ②울타리가 O 뒤로 이어지는 디테일이 두 번째로 무시됨 → "울타리 널빤지가 O의 왼쪽 테두리와 실제로 겹쳐야 한다"는 식으로 더 구체적으로 지시.)*

**CRITICAL FRAMING RULE — read this first: the "O" and "X" letter shapes are the main subject and must stay exactly as large as in a typical quiz-card thumbnail — each shape roughly 40-45% of the frame width, filling most of the vertical frame height, with only a modest margin of sky/ground around them. Do NOT shrink the O and X to make room for background details. Do NOT add extra empty sky or ground padding. The background scenery (fence, tree, park, distant figures) must fit AROUND the still-large O and X, not replace their scale.

A warm, polished children's-book / high-quality webtoon illustration style — NOT generic clip-art, NOT a cheap stock-icon look. Confident but varied linework, not one uniform thick outline. Soft, harmonious, slightly muted color palette (dusty coral, sage green, warm cream, soft gold) with gentle painterly watercolor-style shading.

Wide landscape format for a quiz game card. Two big "O" and "X" shapes dominate most of the frame (see CRITICAL FRAMING RULE above), softly shaded coral for the X and sage green for the O.

Background composition: a low wooden fence runs along the left side of the background. CRITICAL: the fence's horizontal top rail and vertical wooden slats must physically extend rightward until the rightmost slat visually overlaps and is partially occluded by the left edge of the "O" shape's ring — the fence should look like it is still going but gets cut off / hidden behind the O's outline, the same way a real object in front of a fence blocks part of it. Do not let the fence stop in the open grass before reaching the O. Beyond the fence, a peaceful grassy lawn park stretches toward the horizon — keep the park otherwise empty and clean, do NOT add any sculpture, statue, or art installations in the grass. Far on the horizon — small, distant, and quite soft/hazy/blurred — keep the faint impression of a modern city skyline and the long silhouette of a cable-stayed bridge, barely legible through the haze.

The "O" shape has a hole in its center like a real letter O — inside that hole, a small white Korean Jindo dog peeks out, just its head and one paw visible through the opening, the rest hidden behind the O. Short smooth close-lying white coat, lean build, small erect triangular ears, fox-like narrow snout.

In the gap of open lawn visible behind and between the O and X (a small background vignette, NOT taking up much frame space, NOT shrinking the O/X): a small, distant, soft-focus figure of a man running/chasing after a small white dog ahead of him, seen from behind, mid-stride. He wears a black baseball cap, white t-shirt, light overshirt, shorts, sneakers — rendered small enough to read as a background detail, soft and slightly hazy like the distant skyline, not sharply detailed. This pair should sit in the mid-background grass, clearly smaller and further away than the main O/X shapes, without changing the overall size or framing of the O and X.

Overall craft level: warm, tasteful, a little sophisticated — like a well-designed children's app or a polished Korean webtoon watercolor illustration. No text or letters other than the O and X shapes themselves.**
