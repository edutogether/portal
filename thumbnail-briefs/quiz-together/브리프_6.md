# QUIZ TOGETHER 썸네일 — 추가 프롬프트 (배경 스카이라인 흐리게, 10차 수정)

아래 프롬프트를 그대로 이미지 생성 AI에 붙여넣으시면 됩니다.

*(직전 시도는 O/X 크기는 잘 나왔는데, 배경 도시 스카이라인이 또렷한 고층빌딩+경기장 건물까지 선명하게 그려져서 너무 진하고 눈에 띔. 원래 의도는 "저 멀리서 흐릿하게 보이는" 느낌이라, 이번엔 흐림·거리감을 훨씬 더 강하게 명시. 추가로 X를 좀 더 가운데 쪽으로 당기고, 남자는 O/X 사이가 아니라 X 오른쪽으로 위치 이동.)*

---

**CRITICAL FRAMING RULE — read this first: the "O" and "X" letter shapes are the main subject and must stay large, filling most of the frame (roughly 40-45% of the frame width each), with only a modest margin of sky/ground around them. Do NOT shrink the O and X to make room for background details.

A warm, polished children's-book / high-quality webtoon illustration style — NOT generic clip-art, NOT a cheap stock-icon look. Confident but varied linework. Soft, harmonious, slightly muted color palette (dusty coral, sage green, warm cream, soft gold) with gentle painterly watercolor-style shading.

Wide landscape format for a quiz game card. Two big "O" and "X" shapes dominate most of the frame, softly shaded coral for the X and sage green for the O. Composition/positioning: pull the "X" shape inward toward the horizontal center of the frame, closer to the "O" (reduce the empty gap between O and X — they should read as a tighter, more balanced pair near the middle of the frame), leaving a smaller strip of open background visible on the right side beyond the X, and only a small strip of background between O and X.

Background composition: a low wooden fence runs along the left side of the background, its top rail and slats extending rightward until the rightmost slat visually overlaps and is partially hidden behind the left edge of the "O" shape's ring. Beyond the fence, a peaceful grassy lawn park stretches toward the horizon — keep it otherwise empty and clean, no sculptures or statues.

CRITICAL BACKGROUND HAZE RULE: far on the horizon, the city skyline and any structures must be rendered EXTREMELY soft, pale, and low-contrast — like something seen from a great distance through atmospheric haze on a hazy summer day. Reduce contrast and saturation of the skyline drastically (almost blending into the pale sky color), keep silhouettes vague and indistinct with no readable architectural detail (no clearly defined skyscraper windows, no identifiable stadium or building shapes) — it should read as a faint, ghostly impression at the very back of the scene, NOT a crisp, detailed, prominent skyline. Only a long, equally soft and hazy silhouette of a cable-stayed bridge may be included, just as faint. The foreground/midground (fence, tree, grass, O, X) should stay crisp and saturated as normal — the haze applies ONLY to the far background structures.

The "O" shape has a hole in its center like a real letter O — inside that hole, a small white Korean Jindo dog peeks out, just its head and one paw visible through the opening, the rest hidden behind the O. Short smooth close-lying white coat, lean build, small erect triangular ears, fox-like narrow snout.

In the open lawn to the RIGHT of the "X" shape (not between the O and X — that gap should stay small and mostly empty per the composition note above): a small, distant, soft-focus figure of a man running/chasing after a small white dog ahead of him, seen from behind, mid-stride running pose (not walking). He wears a black baseball cap, white t-shirt, light overshirt, shorts, sneakers — rendered small and soft enough to read as a background detail, matching the same soft haze quality as the distant skyline.

Overall craft level: warm, tasteful, a little sophisticated — like a well-designed children's app or a polished Korean webtoon watercolor illustration. No text or letters other than the O and X shapes themselves.**
