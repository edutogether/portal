# QUIZ TOGETHER 썸네일 — 클린 재작성 (15차)

지금까지 조금씩 덧붙이기만 해서 프롬프트가 지저분해진 게 문제였을 수 있어서, 처음부터
깔끔하게 다시 정리했습니다. **새 대화창**에서, 참고 이미지 없이 아래 텍스트만
붙여넣어보세요.

---

**Style**: Warm, polished children's-book / high-quality webtoon watercolor illustration. Soft, muted palette (dusty coral, sage green, warm cream, soft gold). Confident, varied linework — not stiff clip-art.

**Layout (most important — read first)**: Wide landscape quiz-card image. Left-of-center: a large sage-green "O" letter shape. Right-of-center: a large coral "X" letter shape. There should be a clearly visible gap of open background between the O and the X — they must NOT touch or overlap. Each shape takes up roughly 40% of the frame width.

**The O**: has a hole in the middle like a real letter O. A small white Korean Jindo dog peeks through the hole — just head and one paw visible, rest hidden behind the O.

**Left background**: a low wooden fence starts at the far left edge and runs rightward, ending exactly where it touches the O — the last fence post should be partly hidden behind the O's left edge, like the fence continues behind it. A tree stands near the top-left corner.

**Center background (the gap between O and X)**: open grass, and standing in this gap — roughly in the middle, between the two letters — a small white fluffy companion dog (not the Jindo), running.

**Right background (past the X)**: further right, past the X, a man is running — small and distant, seen from behind, chasing after the dog. His running pose must look like an actual athletic runner: front leg bent and lifted, back leg extended behind him, arms swinging opposite to his legs — a clear mid-stride action pose, not standing or walking. He wears a plain black baseball cap (no strap), and a plain white t-shirt with a small simple red graphic printed on the back (a couple of small red dice shapes is fine, but simple and small — it doesn't need to be a specific real logo).

**Far horizon (behind everything)**: a hazy, soft, low-contrast skyline — a cluster of modern skyscrapers plus one large modern stadium building with a curved white roof — rendered pale and blurred like it's far in the distance, with a line of trees in front of it. Keep it soft/hazy, but it should still be recognizably a skyline + stadium, just distant and faded, not removed or replaced.

**Ground**: sandy dirt path under the O and X, transitioning to grass elsewhere. No sculptures, no statues, no extra text anywhere except the O and X shapes themselves.
