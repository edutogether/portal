**A warm, polished children's-book / high-quality webtoon illustration style — NOT generic clip-art, NOT a cheap stock-icon look. Confident but varied linework (not one uniform thick black outline everywhere — line weight should breathe, thinner in some places, bolder in others, like a skilled illustrator's hand). Soft, harmonious, slightly muted color palette (dusty coral, sage green, warm cream, soft gold) with gentle painterly shading and soft color blending — not flat oversaturated primary colors, not a harsh solid fill look.

Wide landscape format for a quiz game card. Two big "O" and "X" shapes dominate most of the frame, softly shaded coral for the X and sage green for the O, elegant and characterful rather than generic. A small, simple, tasteful backyard scene in the background (a hint of fence, a tree, soft clouds) — kept minimal and restrained, not cluttered with generic clip-art stars/flowers/suns.

The "O" shape has a hole in its center (like a real letter O / ring donut shape) — inside that hole, a small white Korean Jindo dog peeks out, as if looking through the ring from behind it: just its head and maybe one paw visible through the opening, the rest of its body hidden behind the O. The dog should read as a Jindo specifically — short smooth close-lying white coat (not fluffy/puffy), lean build, small erect triangular ears, a fox-like narrow snout — drawn small, charming, and easy to miss at a glance, not the main subject.

Overall craft level: warm, tasteful, a little sophisticated — the kind of illustration you'd see in a well-designed children's app or a polished Korean webtoon, not a free stock clip-art pack. No text or letters other than the O and X shapes themselves.**
